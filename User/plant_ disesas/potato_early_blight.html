Potato Early Blight Disease

Learn symptoms, causes, and treatment methods for potato early blight disease.

1. Leaf Early Blight

Plant Identification

Leaf early blight affects potato leaves and spreads quickly in warm humid conditions.

Symptoms

  • Brown circular spots on leaves
  • Yellow edges around spots
  • Drying lower leaves
  • Leaf dropping

How to Take Care

  • Remove infected leaves
  • Avoid overhead watering
  • Apply fungicide spray
  • Maintain proper spacing

2. Stem Blight

Plant Identification

Stem blight damages potato stems and weakens the overall plant growth.

Symptoms

  • Dark stem lesions
  • Weak stems
  • Wilting branches
  • Slow plant growth

How to Take Care

  • Use healthy seed potatoes
  • Improve air circulation
  • Keep field clean
  • Use balanced fertilizers

3. Tuber Blight

Plant Identification

Tuber blight infects potato tubers and reduces storage quality.

Symptoms

  • Dark sunken spots on tubers
  • Soft rotting areas
  • Bad odor in storage
  • Reduced potato quality

How to Take Care

  • Store potatoes in dry places
  • Remove infected tubers
  • Use disease-free seeds
  • Maintain clean storage area

4. Collar Rot

Plant Identification

Collar rot affects the lower stem area near the soil surface.

Symptoms

  • Brown stem base
  • Plant wilting
  • Yellow leaves
  • Weak plant growth

How to Take Care

  • Avoid waterlogging
  • Use raised beds
  • Apply fungicide treatment
  • Remove infected plants

5. Severe Early Blight

Plant Identification

Severe early blight spreads rapidly and causes major crop loss in potato fields.

Symptoms

  • Large dark leaf spots
  • Complete leaf drying
  • Poor tuber growth
  • Low crop yield

How to Take Care

  • Rotate crops regularly
  • Apply fungicides on time
  • Monitor plants regularly
  • Remove infected debris
