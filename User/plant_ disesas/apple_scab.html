Apple Scab Disease

Learn symptoms, causes, and treatment methods for apple scab disease.

1. Leaf Scab

Plant Identification

Leaf scab is a fungal disease that mainly affects apple leaves during wet weather.

Symptoms

  • Olive green spots on leaves
  • Dark rough patches
  • Yellowing leaves
  • Early leaf drop

How to Take Care

  • Remove infected leaves
  • Improve air circulation
  • Use fungicide spray
  • Keep orchard clean

2. Fruit Scab

Plant Identification

Fruit scab infects apple fruits and reduces market quality.

Symptoms

  • Dark rough spots on fruits
  • Cracked apple skin
  • Deformed fruits
  • Reduced fruit quality

How to Take Care

  • Use disease-resistant varieties
  • Spray fungicides regularly
  • Prune crowded branches
  • Remove infected fruits

3. Twig Scab

Plant Identification

Twig scab damages young shoots and weakens apple tree growth.

Symptoms

  • Dark lesions on twigs
  • Dry young shoots
  • Weak branch growth
  • Poor flowering

How to Take Care

  • Prune infected twigs
  • Use clean gardening tools
  • Maintain proper spacing
  • Apply protective sprays

4. Severe Apple Scab

Plant Identification

Severe apple scab spreads quickly during rainy seasons and causes major damage.

Symptoms

  • Heavy black spots
  • Complete leaf damage
  • Fruit cracking
  • Reduced harvest

How to Take Care

  • Monitor orchard regularly
  • Apply fungicide on time
  • Remove infected debris
  • Maintain tree health

5. Early Stage Apple Scab

Plant Identification

Early stage apple scab appears as small spots before spreading across the tree.

Symptoms

  • Small olive spots
  • Mild leaf discoloration
  • Young fruit infection
  • Slow spread at early stage

How to Take Care

  • Detect disease early
  • Use preventive sprays
  • Keep trees properly pruned
  • Provide balanced nutrients
