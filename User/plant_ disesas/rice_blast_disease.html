Rice Blast Disease Identification

Learn symptoms, causes, and treatment methods for rice blast diseases.

1. Leaf Blast

Plant Identification

Leaf blast is a common fungal disease that affects rice leaves during humid weather.

Symptoms

  • Diamond-shaped spots on leaves
  • Gray center with brown borders
  • Drying leaf tips
  • Reduced plant growth

How to Take Care

  • Use disease-resistant seeds
  • Avoid excess nitrogen fertilizer
  • Maintain proper water level
  • Apply fungicide spray

2. Node Blast

Plant Identification

Node blast attacks the stem nodes of rice plants and weakens plant structure.

Symptoms

  • Black or brown stem nodes
  • Weak stem structure
  • Broken stems
  • Poor grain formation

How to Take Care

  • Keep field clean
  • Remove infected plants
  • Improve field drainage
  • Use balanced fertilizers

3. Neck Blast

Plant Identification

Neck blast affects the neck portion of rice panicles and reduces grain yield.

Symptoms

  • Brown neck region
  • Empty grains
  • Dry panicles
  • Reduced harvest

How to Take Care

  • Spray fungicides early
  • Use healthy seeds
  • Maintain proper spacing
  • Monitor crop regularly

4. Collar Blast

Plant Identification

Collar blast appears where the leaf joins the stem and damages rice leaves.

Symptoms

  • Brown lesions at leaf base
  • Leaf drying
  • Weak leaf attachment
  • Yellowing leaves

How to Take Care

  • Maintain field hygiene
  • Use resistant rice varieties
  • Avoid overcrowding plants
  • Provide proper irrigation

5. Panicle Blast

Plant Identification

Panicle blast infects rice grains and affects grain quality and production.

Symptoms

  • Discolored grains
  • Dry panicles
  • Poor grain filling
  • Low crop yield

How to Take Care

  • Apply recommended fungicides
  • Use certified seeds
  • Maintain balanced nutrients
  • Remove infected crop residue
