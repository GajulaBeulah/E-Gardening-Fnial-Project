Root Rot Disease Identification

Learn symptoms, causes, and treatment methods for root rot diseases.

1. Root Rot

Plant Identification

Root rot is caused by excess water and fungal infection that damages plant roots.

Symptoms

  • Brown and mushy roots
  • Yellow leaves
  • Wilting plant
  • Slow growth

How to Take Care

  • Reduce watering
  • Use well-drained soil
  • Remove damaged roots
  • Apply fungicide

2. Pythium Root Rot

Plant Identification

Pythium root rot commonly affects indoor and greenhouse plants in wet soil conditions.

Symptoms

  • Soft black roots
  • Drooping leaves
  • Weak stems
  • Plant collapse

How to Take Care

  • Avoid overwatering
  • Improve drainage
  • Use clean pots
  • Remove infected plants

3. Phytophthora Root Rot

Plant Identification

This disease spreads in wet and poorly drained soil causing severe root damage.

Symptoms

  • Dark root decay
  • Leaf yellowing
  • Stem discoloration
  • Poor fruit growth

How to Take Care

  • Keep soil dry
  • Use raised beds
  • Apply fungicide treatment
  • Improve airflow

4. Fusarium Root Rot

Plant Identification

Fusarium fungus attacks roots and blocks water movement inside plants.

Symptoms

  • Dry brown roots
  • Wilting leaves
  • Stunted growth
  • Leaf dropping

How to Take Care

  • Use healthy soil
  • Rotate crops regularly
  • Remove infected roots
  • Use resistant plants

5. Black Root Rot

Plant Identification

Black root rot causes dark root infection and affects nutrient absorption.

Symptoms

  • Blackened roots
  • Yellow leaves
  • Poor plant health
  • Reduced flowering

How to Take Care

  • Keep soil clean
  • Avoid waterlogging
  • Use fungicide spray
  • Remove infected plants
